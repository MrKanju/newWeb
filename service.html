Interior Design

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do you offer end-to-end interior solutions?

    Yes. We handle the full process from design consultation and planning to manufacturing, material delivery, installation, and final handover—so you get a smooth, single-team experience.

  • Why should I choose customized interiors instead of ready-made furniture?

    Customized interiors offer better fit, storage, finishes, and satisfaction. You can:

    • * Choose materials, colors, and fittings
    • * Match your preferred style
    • * Optimize difficult spaces
    • * Control the budget at design stage

  • How does the interior design process work at Vismaya?

    • * Consultation & requirement discussion
    • * Site visit + measurements
    • * Detailed drawings & estimate
    • * Design approval
    • * Production
    • * Delivery & execution
    • * On-time handover

  • Will I get detailed drawings before work starts?

    Yes. We share detailed drawings and layouts for approval before production begins.

  • Can I modify the design based on my budget?

    Yes. You can adjust materials, finishes, and scope during the design stage to match your budget.

  • Do you help with space planning and functionality?

    Yes. We focus on smart planning, smooth movement, storage optimization, and practical layouts.

  • Do you take measurements at the site?

    Yes. We take actual site measurements and may re-check before installation to avoid mismatch.

  • How do you ensure quality and customer satisfaction?

    • * Systematic workflow
    • * Quality materials and fittings
    • * Proper planning and supervision
    • * Final inspection and handover after customer satisfaction
